# Glimdock enclosure

The current public design is [Portable r45](public/portable-r45/README.md):
a sculpted removable display pod with a matching full rear shell, internal
battery space and two speakers. Separate landscape and portrait desk stands
support the pod without fastening it to the base.

The curated release contains 23 current printable parts, editable STEP/BREP,
an original-parts assembly, a self-contained interactive viewer and portable
OrcaSlicer layouts. It includes the repaired front and matching USB-C tunnel,
two carrier rails, one-piece spring button, closed-front stand and stronger
keyed accessories. Development trials, printer connections, G-code and
manufacturer PCB CAD are excluded.

The supported V1 display's landscape layout is 320 × 240, with the USB connector
to the right. A portrait stand is a mechanical option; it does not establish
portrait support in an installed firmware version.

Use the package's assembly, print and validation notes. These remain prototype
files: nominal CAD and historical toolpath checks do not qualify every physical
fit, surface finish, spring lifetime or battery configuration. Select your own
printer and filament, then slice and review the public layouts locally.

The earlier [Pebble r26 snapshot](public/pebble-r26-prototype-v2/README.md) and
`pebble-landscape/` parametric source remain historical references. Their hatch,
carrier, saddle and angled-print instructions do not describe r45.
