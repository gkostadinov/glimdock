# Glimdock enclosure

The current design is [Pebble Landscape](pebble-landscape/README.md), revision
`r19-concentric-exact-surface-fit-landscape-pebble`: a purple pillowed body above
a low white USB-powered base, tilted back 18 degrees. The 320 × 240 display uses
USB-right landscape rotation 3. A concentric 2 mm front border exposes the complete
factory glass, including its wider right-hand black chin.

The side USB-C connector uses a provisional internal extension. Complete cable
fit and assembled power/data operation still need physical validation. The
factory boot/reset buttons require opening the enclosure. The larger battery
base is a future variant, not part of the current main model.

The purple body was sent for a one-part prototype print on 9 October 2026.
That print does not establish that the display, inserts, posts or USB extension
fit. The case-design guide remains the authoritative assembly and validation
record. Firmware and browser update behavior are documented in the root SETUP guide.

The earlier Serein, portrait Pebble, sculpted and round concepts remain in their
existing directories as historical work; they are not the current case.
