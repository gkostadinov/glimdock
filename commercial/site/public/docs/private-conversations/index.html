Find a conversation.
Follow the exchange.

Choose a session.

Browse recent conversations with provider, machine and source timestamp. Twelve sessions per page keep the display responsive.

Read it as a chat.

User and assistant bubbles show the exchange in source order. Scroll through paragraphs, headings and framed code, then move to the next five messages.

Continue longer messages.

A “Read more” control loads the next bounded part of a long message. Previous-page controls help you return to the context.

Open the conversation from its task.

In Agents, choose Tasks, open a task and tap Open conversation. Select one of its linked chats; it opens at the newest message. Scroll up to read earlier history and use Back to return to the task. Tasks without an archive entry say so clearly. The full archive remains under Settings → Private conversations.

Pair a reader that belongs to you.

The optional reader uses an authenticated HTTPS relay with a separate, revocable credential for each display. Provision the relay origin, credential and CA certificate over USB; the display verifies the certificate before requesting a page.

Your archive collection and relay deployment use your chosen private implementation. The firmware includes the bounded private-chat/v1 client and USB helper; it does not include a hosted conversation archive.

Pairing and displayed text have different lifetimes.The credential and CA remain in device settings. Conversation text stays in a separate RAM model and clears when you leave the view. The device does not execute commands from messages.

Private runtime. Public source.

Separate from monitoring.
