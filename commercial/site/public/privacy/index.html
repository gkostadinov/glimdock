YOUR DATA. YOUR CHOICE.

Privacy & cookies.

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Glimdock’s website is operated by Georgi Kostadinov. Contact support@glimdock.com with privacy questions. Local device monitoring is separate: your homelab readings stay on your network and are not sent to this website.

What the website uses

Essential functions serve these pages, remember your cookie choices in this browser and protect checkout. During checkout, this tab remembers quantity, destination, requested body and stand colours, and a request identifier so a retry can recover the same checkout. Contact, delivery-address and card fields are collected by Stripe. The site uses OpenAI Sites and Cloudflare for hosting and delivery. They may process basic request and security logs, such as IP address and browser information, to serve and protect the website.

For any historical paid orders, Stripe hosts the payment form and processes payment, contact and delivery information needed to complete your order. Glimdock receives order details needed for fulfillment, support, accounting and consumer rights. Card details are handled by Stripe. Cloudflare Email Service processes recipient addresses and order or support-message content to send transactional confirmations and route incoming support mail; these messages are separate from optional analytics.

Unpaid interest and availability emails

If you register interest, we process your email address and the quantity, country and body/stand colour preferences you choose to provide. We use them to confirm your signup, understand interest and email you about Glimdock availability. The legal basis is your explicit consent through the unchecked signup box and email confirmation. Registration is free and creates no order, reservation, payment or guaranteed price or delivery date.

We send a confirmation link that expires after seven days. Unconfirmed signup records are deleted after seven days. Confirmed signups are kept for up to twelve calendar months from confirmation, unless you withdraw consent earlier. Every availability email includes an unsubscribe link; you can also request deletion by emailing support@glimdock.com. Withdrawing consent stops future availability emails without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

Cloudflare hosts the signup records and processes recipient addresses and email content to send confirmation and availability emails. Email addresses and confirmation or unsubscribe credentials are not stored in browser storage or sent to analytics. Link credentials are removed from the address bar before other page scripts run; opening a link alone does not confirm or unsubscribe you.

Optional analytics and advertising

Analytics help us understand visits and improve the website. Advertising measurement helps us understand campaigns and, where enabled, personalize ads. These are separate choices. Neither is required to browse or register interest. Optional tracking does not load before you opt in.

Tracking configurationOptional tracking stays off until you consent. Cookie settings show the services currently configured.

If Google Analytics is enabled after your consent, it can process page visits, browser/device information and a pseudonymous browser identifier. Google may process data outside the EEA using applicable transfer safeguards. User and event retention is set to two months, without resetting that period on new activity; aggregate reports may be kept longer. Ad personalization, Google Signals and detailed location/device collection are disabled. We do not send order addresses, emails or homelab readings to analytics. Google’s privacy policy explains its processing.

Change your mind at any time

Your choice is stored locally with a timestamp and policy version for up to six months. A material tracking change asks for a new choice. Rejecting optional tracking keeps the website and interest form usable. Withdrawing consent stops future tracking from this site; it does not undo processing that has already happened.

Why we process data

Optional analytics and advertising rely on your consent. Order handling and purchase-related support use the information needed to perform your contract. Applicable accounting and consumer duties can require recordkeeping. Basic website security protects our legitimate interest in running a reliable service. We do not send withdrawal forms or customer contact details to analytics.

Contact, retention and your rights

Support correspondence is used to handle your request. Order information is retained for fulfillment and the periods required for accounting and consumer obligations. You can request access, correction or erasure, object to relevant processing, or withdraw consent by contacting support. You may also complain to the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection.

Paid checkout is currently closed. Interest signup does not request a delivery address or payment information. Existing order, support and statutory-rights records remain separate from the availability list.
