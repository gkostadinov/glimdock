Glimdock browser demo

The canvas runs the production Glimdock C++ firmware UI and snapshot parser with LVGL 9.3.0, compiled to WebAssembly. The renderer files are copied without modification from collector-web/emulator; emulator/manifest.json records the source and binary hashes.

All four nodes and all readings are synthetic. The transport generates them in browser memory, makes no collector or LAN requests, and has no credentials. Node configuration is read-only in this public demo. Display settings are temporary and reset with the demo.
