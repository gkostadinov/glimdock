ONE POD. TWO WAYS TO PLACE IT.

The stand stays simple.
The display comes with you.

01 / BODY

Two shells. Useful space inside.

The matching front and rear enclose the display, two carrier rails, a fixed battery tray and space for two speakers. Four rear screws keep the internals accessible. There is no separate battery hatch.

02 / STANDS

Landscape or portrait.

Separate white stands cradle the housing without locking it down. The landscape stand has a continuous front edge and a hidden button-clearance well. Lift the pod out when you need to reach its BAT_PWR button.

03 / ACCESSORIES

A different character.

Swap between white bunny ears, TV antennas and eyes. Keyed sockets work in either orientation; four curved plugs close the unused sockets. The current stems have shallow grip ribs to improve retention.

The portrait stand is a mechanical option. Its printed fit and a matching portrait firmware layout need their own checks. Accessory grip and wear depend on the actual print.

The supported display board.

The working firmware targets the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2.8 V1, with ST7789 display, CST328 touch, 16 MiB flash and 8 MiB PSRAM. The case exposes the board’s own USB-C port. V2 uses a different touch controller; the V1 image and this enclosure do not establish V2 compatibility.

A button you can install from inside.

The current BAT_PWR button is one printed piece with small return springs and fixed glue tabs. Dry-fit it against the actual board switch, then bond only its fixed inside-wall tabs. Keep the spring arms free so the switch can click and return. The two carrier rails locate the display using its factory mounting posts.

Battery and speakers belong in the pod.

The mechanical layout reserves a 60.5 × 50.2 × 7.4 mm battery envelope and two 30 × 20 × 4.7 mm speaker envelopes. The battery tray fixes to the rear shell; speaker frames sit beside the rear grilles. Padding, a soft retaining strap and clear lead routes are part of assembly.

USB remains the starting point for setup. Before battery operation, check the actual pack, board, connector polarity and charging configuration. The measured space does not qualify an interchangeable battery or its electrical setup. Firmware battery indication is approximate voltage, not a runtime or charging-state guarantee.

Print, fit and service.

The source work used PLA with a 0.4 mm nozzle. Review the notes for support placement and clear fitting surfaces carefully. The accessory layout includes an 8 mm outside brim on every piece. Personal printer profiles, connection data, macros and G-code are excluded from the portable layouts.
