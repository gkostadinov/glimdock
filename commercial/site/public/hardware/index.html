Current CAD and assembly notes ↗

The supported display board.

Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-LCD-2.8 V1, with ST7789 display, CST328 touch, 16 MiB flash and 8 MiB PSRAM. The current case uses USB-right landscape at 320 × 240, rotation 3. V2 uses a different touch controller and is not supported by the V1 image.

Current case, explicit evidence.

The fitted r25 body uses the board’s native USB opening, a continuous white cradle and compatible internal components. The r26 body recipe changes print orientation and removable supports while preserving the fitted CAD. Complete assembly and a successful print are physical checks, separate from CAD review.

Prototype, not a production-readiness claim.Use the public enclosure guide for authoritative part provenance and fit caveats. Personal slicer profiles, printer addresses, dispatch logs, G-code and manufacturer reference geometry are not redistributed as project source.

USB first; optional board battery features.

The published case direction is USB-powered. Firmware can show an attached supported LiPo’s approximate voltage, but that is not a validated battery enclosure, charging-state detector or accurate fuel gauge.

Service and rebuild.

Keep the supported board and data-capable cable accessible during firmware setup. Source, public update images, matching relink material and license notices are available. Check your actual board revision before printing or flashing.
